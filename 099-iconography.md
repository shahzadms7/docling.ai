# Iconography Standard

Use a coherent local icon system with consistent stroke/weight, sizing and meaning. Prefer simple universal symbols and text labels for critical actions.

Rules:
- no remote icon CDN;
- icons are decorative unless they convey unique meaning;
- icon-only buttons require accessible labels/tooltips;
- destructive, warning, success and information symbols keep consistent semantics;
- do not use vendor logos as operational icons;
- document-type icons may use neutral W/X/P/PDF-style shorthand without implying vendor affiliation;
- status indicators always include text.
