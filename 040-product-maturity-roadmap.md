# Product Maturity Roadmap

## Gate A — Foundation
Naming, deployment state machine, safety boundary, lifecycle schema, diagnostics, synthetic fixtures, release integrity.

## Gate B — Deterministic Corpus
DOCX/XLSX/PPTX/PDF/image/code extraction, structural manifests, reconciliation, duplicates, lifecycle/history.

## Gate C — Retrieval
Metadata/exact, SQLite FTS, embeddings, hybrid ranking, citation coordinates, regression queries.

## Gate D — Local AI
Ollama profiles, local benchmark, grounded chat, prompt-injection controls, session trace.

## Gate E — Command Center
Upload/intake, queue, exceptions, search, chat, generation, health, audit and recovery.

## Gate F — Generation
Governed DOCX/XLSX/PPTX/PDF/code/diagram generation with validation and human review.

## Gate G — Pilot
Monitoring, backup/restore, migration, performance, failure injection, operator runbooks.

## Gate H — Enterprise
Organization-specific security integration, change governance, DR objectives, supply-chain policy, accessibility, lifecycle and release certification.

Unknown future risks are handled through threat/failure-register expansion and regression tests; no design can truthfully guarantee discovery of every unforeseeable condition.
